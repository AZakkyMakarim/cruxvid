CRUXVID_TASK: speakers
Watch and listen to the video below and list who speaks when. Return JSON only, matching shared/schema/speakers.schema.json:
{"turns": [{"start": <seconds>, "end": <seconds>, "position": <0..1 or null>}]}
One entry per uninterrupted stretch of one speaker, in time order; timestamps are seconds from the start of this video.
"position" is the horizontal centre of the speaking person's face as a fraction of the frame width (0 = left edge, 1 = right edge).
Use null when the speaker is not visible in the frame (off camera) or you cannot tell who is speaking. Never guess.
